Senior Consultant and Co-founder

Ma. Theresa B. Ramos

MA, RPSY, ACC, ACMC, CHt, CMC, CSFP, CFBA

As co-founder of Premier, Thresa leads the firm’s family strategy and the design of its training programs, guided by experiential education, strengths-based methods and servant leadership.

About Thresa

Thresa designs training modules and facilitates group and family dynamics sessions. She runs the firm’s family programs, including Family Culture Building and the Family Constitution retreat.

She has served more than 80 Filipino family businesses across the Philippines and abroad, and believes she is called to help families build stronger relationships for the holistic growth of the individual, the family and the enterprise.

Credentials

  • Certified in Family Business Advising by the Family Firm Institute, Boston, USA
  • Master of Arts in Clinical Psychology, University of San Carlos
  • Certificate in Family Dynamics and Counseling, Center for Family Ministries (CEFAM), Ateneo de Manila University

Organizational Systems Effectiveness (OSE)

Professionalizing the family business so it outlasts the founder

Leadership development, strategic and operational planning, and human resource management for family-owned companies.

Get startedTalk to a consultant

In short

What does professionalizing a family business mean?

Professionalizing a family business means replacing decisions that depend on one person’s memory and presence with systems anyone competent can run. In practice it covers defined roles and reporting lines, written policies for hiring and promotion that apply to relatives too, financial reporting the family can read, delegated authority with clear limits, and performance review for family and non-family staff on the same terms.

What the program covers

The founder is the bottleneck, and everybody knows it.

  • Leadership development for family and non-family managers
  • Strategic and operational planning
  • Roles, reporting lines and delegated authority
  • Human resource systems that apply to relatives on the same terms

A business that only runs on one person cannot be handed to anyone.

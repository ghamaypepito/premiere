About Premier

We honor family unity as the cornerstone of an enduring legacy.

Your business represents a lifetime of dedication and a gift for the generations that follow. It is our privilege to help you align shared values, so that both the enterprise and the family grow stronger.

Our mission

We inspire and influence family unity as the highest model for building generational wealth in family business.

Our vision

The Global Family Enterprise Resource Center for Excellence: a trusted platform for business families who want to strengthen unity, preserve legacy, and build wealth that lasts beyond one generation.

Our core values

How we think, serve, decide and build trust.

Families entrust us with matters that are deeply personal, generational and lasting. These four values shape every engagement.

Positivity

Optimism, flexibility, collaboration and a constructive spirit brought to every challenge.

Responsibility

Proactive service, dependability, organization and resourcefulness in delivering meaningful outcomes.

God-centered

Humility, patience, commitment and faith shaping our work and our relationships.

Integrity

Honesty, sincerity, transparency and confidentiality, above all else.

Milestones

More than a decade of work with families in business.

From one conference in Cebu to more than 100 family businesses across the Philippines and abroad.

2011

The first Family Enterprise Excellence Conference

Premier convened its first Family Enterprise Excellence Conference in Cebu City, bringing business families together to learn from each other.

2014

A network of 23 professionals

Premier grew to a partnership of at least 23 professionals from different fields and disciplines, committed to serving, strengthening and uniting families in business.

2015

Partnerships with chambers of commerce

Co-organized family business seminar-workshops with the Chambers of Commerce and Industry of Cebu City, Mandaue City, Cagayan de Oro City, Davao City and General Santos City.

2018

Five national conferences

The Family Enterprise Excellence Conference returned to Cebu City for the fourth time, after Cebu editions in 2011, 2013 and 2015 and a Mindanao edition in General Santos in 2016.

2021

Family Firm Institute Fellowship

Jon Ramos was granted Fellow status by the Family Firm Institute in October 2021, and became the first Filipino on its Board of Directors. Premier also hosted the virtual Asian Family Enterprise Excellence Conference, with over 700 registrants worldwide.

2023

More than 100 family businesses

Premier had served more than 100 families in business, across retail, fishing, food, real estate, education, transportation, energy, agribusiness and hospitality, in Luzon, the Visayas, Mindanao and two countries abroad.

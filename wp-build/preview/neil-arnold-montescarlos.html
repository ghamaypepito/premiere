Executive Director and Chief Operations Officer

Neil Arnold C. Montescarlos

CFBA

Neil has over 30 years of leadership experience. He specializes in guiding pioneering and start-up organizations, with a strong focus on family business governance, family dynamics facilitation and leadership development.

About Neil

His years in the seminary honed his skills in personal and group-learning facilitation, processing and coaching. Extensive experience in management and administration made him an expert in visioning, goal-setting, strategic planning and implementation, and in developing people through module design and motivational speaking.

His direct approach to management lets him get to the root of an issue and put the right systems in place promptly.

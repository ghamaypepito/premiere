CEO and Founder

Jonathan “Jon” A. Ramos

FFI Fellow, ACFWA

“The family business is everybody’s business.”

Jon Ramos founded Premier Family Business Consulting to help families build long-lasting businesses, by introducing the tools and sciences of family business succession to the family.

About Jon

His journey began when families in business approached him for advice and mentoring. That led him to study the sciences of family business, and to build a firm of value-driven professionals committed to uniting families in business.

He believes building strong competencies across an organization is a must, and that knowing how to innovate and manage change has become urgent. By his own count he has served more than 80 families in business.

Credentials

  • First Filipino on the Family Firm Institute Board of Directors, 2021
  • ACFWA from the Family Firm Institute
  • Pioneer graduate of the University of Asia and the Pacific and IESE Advanced Management Program

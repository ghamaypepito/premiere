Premier Family Business Consulting, Inc. · Cebu City

A brand about continuity should look like it will last.

This book documents the identity Premier already owns, tightens the parts that were drifting, and sets the rules for the succession-led site. Every color and every asset here was recovered from the firm's own published brand, not invented for it.

01

The mark

A gold pentagon drawn as five interlocking strokes, five points, and a radiating burst. Five members, one enclosed shape, light coming off it. Do not redraw it.

Primary. White wordmark, gold mark, on Navy 700. This is the default lockup and should be the one used almost everywhere.

Mono. Single-color Navy 700 for light grounds, print, faxable documents and any partner deck that cannot carry the full lockup.

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space = height of the mark

Nothing enters the dashed boundary: no text, no rules, no photo edges. Minimum reproduction width is 120 px on screen and 32 mm in print. Below that, use the pentagon mark alone.

What breaks it

The wordmark is set in a bold italic serif and the descriptor in a bold sans. That pairing is already correct and is the reason the type system in section 03 introduces a serif rather than replacing the wordmark with something geometric. The logo file is artwork, not type — never reset the words in a live font.

Typography

Poppins stays — the firm already owns it and it sets clean interface text. Newsreader joins it for display, because its italic answers the italic serif already sitting inside the wordmark.

Display 1Newsreader 400 · 50/52 · −2.2%
One generation built it.
Display 2Newsreader 500 · 31/36 · −1.5%
Six steps, in this order, every time.
QuoteNewsreader italic 400 · 23/30
My siblings and I cannot agree on what fair actually means.
Body 1Poppins 400 · 17/27.5
Family business succession planning is the process of deciding, documenting and rehearsing how leadership and ownership will pass to the next generation.
Body 2Poppins 400 · 15/24
Confidential interviews with every family member and the senior non-family executives.
LabelPoppins 600 · 11.5 · +15%
The Premier method
  • —Newsreader is for display only. Headlines, pull quotes, the numerals on the six steps. Never for interface labels or anything under 20 px.
  • —Running text stays near 65 characters. Wider lines lose the reader on exactly the pages where losing them costs the most.
  • —Headlines get text-wrap: balance. A one-word orphan on the last line of an H1 undoes the whole page.
  • —Sentence case everywhere except labels. Title Case On Every Heading reads as a brochure from 2009.
  • —Both faces load from Google Fonts. Ship a real fallback stack: Georgia for Newsreader, Segoe UI for Poppins.
04

The pentagon device

The mark's geometry, extracted and reused at scale. This gives the brand a graphic language of its own instead of borrowing stock shapes.

Outline. Section markers and empty states.

Lattice. Generational repetition. Backgrounds at low opacity.

Burst. Sparingly. One per page at most.

The pentagon is never decoration for its own sake. It marks a section, holds an empty state, or sits at low opacity behind a dark band. It never appears at full gold on a light ground, where it competes directly with the primary button.

05

Photography & film

The firm sells judgment about private matters. The imagery has to feel observed rather than staged, and it has to be recognisably Filipino.

The hero film

The Long Table

A slow dolly along an empty narra table in a heritage house, late light through capiz shutters, chairs from three different decades around it.

Eight seconds, looping, muted, navy overlay at 55 percent with a gradient at the foot so the headline always clears. It opens on a poster frame, so the first paint is never black. Under reduced-motion or a slow connection it holds on a still.

Do

  • —Narra, capiz, rattan, concrete, brass. Local materials, not generic corporate glass.
  • —Late afternoon light, low and raking, with visible air.
  • —Navy in the shadows, gold in the highlights. Grade every asset to the palette.
  • —Rooms that were recently occupied. Absence carries the subject better than a posed group.

Never

  • —Stock handshakes, stacked-hands circles, or a group in matching polo shirts.
  • —Cool blue-grey office stock. It reads as an outsourcing firm.
  • —A photograph of a person presented as a client, ever, unless that person is a client who signed a release.
  • —Text baked into an image. It cannot be translated, indexed, or read aloud.

On generated imagery. Everything in the current comps is a placeholder standing in for photography Premier will supply. Generated faces are fine while the design is being reviewed. They must not survive into the live site as clients, consultants, or testimonials, because a firm selling discretion cannot afford to be caught inventing the people it claims to have helped.

06

Motion

Transform and opacity only. Enter on ease-out, spring anything interruptible, and let every motion originate from the thing the visitor touched. Press replay on any of these.

380ms · 60ms stagger
420ms · scaleX
130ms · press 0.972Click it
  • —Never animate layout. Width, height, top and margin force the browser to re-measure the page. Move things with transform and fade them with opacity.
  • —Enter fast, exit faster. 200 to 320 ms in, around 150 ms out. Anything past 400 ms feels like the site is thinking.
  • —One curve. cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1) for entrances, a spring for anything a person can interrupt mid-flight.
  • —Motion has an origin. A panel opens from the control that opened it. Things do not arrive from nowhere.
  • —Everything is visible at rest. No section waits at zero opacity for a scroll listener. If the script fails, the page still reads.
  • —Honour prefers-reduced-motion. Cut the animation, keep the end state, swap the hero film for its still.
07

Voice

Plain, warm, and unafraid of the hard subject. Succession writing usually tiptoes around death, money and fairness. Premier's names them.

We write

If something happened to your father tomorrow, would anyone know who signs?

Not

We provide holistic solutions for business continuity contingencies.

We write

Decide who leads and who owns while the founder is still in the room.

Not

Leveraging our proven framework to unlock generational value.

We write

Forty-five minutes, no cost, and nothing leaves the room.

Not

Contact us today to learn more about our services!

  • —Say the difficult word. Death, illness, money, fairness, resentment. A family already knows why they are on the page.
  • —Second person, active voice. "You decide", not "decisions are made".
  • —No exclamation marks, anywhere. Not in a headline, not in a form confirmation.
  • —Never claim a number the firm has not verified. Bracket it and ship the page without it rather than round up.
  • —English is the default; Filipino and Cebuano appear where a family would actually switch. Never as decoration.

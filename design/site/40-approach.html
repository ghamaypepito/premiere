The Premier method

Six steps, expanded.

01

Listen

[2 to 4 weeks]

Every family member is interviewed alone, including those outside the business and those who married in. Senior non-family executives too, because they see what relatives cannot say.

02

Map

[2 weeks]

Family, ownership and business drawn as three overlapping systems on a single page. Most families have never seen their own.

03

Align

[2 to 4 months]

Facilitated sessions, off site, until agreement is real rather than polite. This is the longest part and the one that decides whether any of the rest holds.

04

Document

[4 to 8 weeks]

Constitution, succession plan, governance charter. Drafted with counsel and signed by everyone they bind.

05

Install

[1 to 2 months]

The family council and the board are convened, chaired, and taught to run without us in the room.

06

Accompany

Ongoing

Quarterly counsel through the handover, and a standing line for the moments nobody planned for.

Every duration here is bracketed until Premier confirms its real averages. Published as HowTo structured data.

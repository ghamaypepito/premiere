The stakes

Roughly a third of family businesses reach the second generation. Around one in eight reach the third.

The ones that survive are rarely the ones with the best product. They are the ones that decided early, and in writing, who leads, who owns, and how the family will handle disagreement.

The Premier method

Six steps, in this order, every time.

The single biggest thing missing from the current site. Families buy a process they can picture themselves inside.

01

Listen

Confidential interviews with every family member and the senior non-family executives.

02

Map

The family, the ownership and the business, drawn side by side so the overlaps become visible.

03

Align

Facilitated family sessions until there is genuine agreement on vision, values and rules.

04

Document

Succession plan, family constitution, governance charter. Written down and signed.

05

Install

Councils, boards and review cycles that keep the agreement alive after we leave.

06

Accompany

Quarterly counsel through the transition itself, which is where most plans quietly fail.

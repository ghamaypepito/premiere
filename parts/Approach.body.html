The Premier method

Six steps, expanded.

01

Listen

[2 to 4 weeks]

Every family member is interviewed alone, including those outside the business and those who married in. Senior non-family executives too.

02

Map

[2 weeks]

Family, ownership and business drawn as three overlapping systems on a single page. Most families have never seen theirs.

03

Align

[2 to 4 months]

Facilitated sessions, off site, until agreement is real rather than polite. This is the longest and most valuable part.

04

Document

[4 to 8 weeks]

Constitution, succession plan, governance charter. Drafted with counsel, signed by everyone it binds.

05

Install

[1 to 2 months]

The family council and the board are convened, chaired and taught to run without us.

06

Accompany

Ongoing

Quarterly counsel through the handover, and a standing line for the moments nobody planned for.
